# Veyora — Brand Identity & Product Naming

## Selected Product Name: **Veyora**

- **Meaning**: Derived from "Velocity" + "Aura" — representing fast, seamless media acquisition with a premium feel.
- **Branding Rationale**: Short, memorable, modern, and distinct from any existing media downloader trademark. Easy to pronounce across languages.
- **Suggested Tagline**: "Download. Convert. Create. — The Professional Media Studio."
- **App Identifier**: `veyora`
- **Config Directory**: `~/.veyora`
- **Default Download Directory**: `~/Downloads/Veyora`

## Visual Identity
- **Primary Color**: Deep Blue (#3b8ed0) — trust, professionalism
- **Accent Color**: Emerald Green (#2ecc71) — success, completion
- **Error Color**: Red (#e74c3c) — failures, cancellations
- **Warning Color**: Orange (#f39c12) — retries, warnings
- **Theme**: Dark Mode by default, with Light and System options
- **Typography**: Clean, modern sans-serif (CTkFont)
- **Icon**: Lightning bolt (⚡) symbolizing speed and power

## Trademark Considerations
- "Veyora" does not conflict with any known media downloader trademark.
- The name is distinct from "4K Video Downloader" and other competitors.
- Suitable for future website, icon, and software installer branding.
